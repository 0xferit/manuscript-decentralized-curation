## Description

Ostwald's color system, with its unique 24-hue color wheel, was very popular in the 1930s and 1940s. So what happened?

The English schools also saw a lot of adoption of the Ostwald system in the 1930s. And this is Judson's _Handbook of Color_, again printed in England. And again, the Ostwald circle with 24 standard hues.

In the 1930s and 40s, the key promoter of Ostwald's system in the United States was Egert Jacobson. Here's the 24 standard hues in his book.
