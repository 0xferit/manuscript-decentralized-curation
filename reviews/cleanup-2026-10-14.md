## Remaining decisions and limits

The research is ready for review, not certified as an implemented or empirically validated mechanism. The [companion question register](../projects/rpgf/companion-paper/source-and-open-questions.md) retains the unresolved accrual, holdback, authorization, zero-weight allocation and implementation-interface questions. The foundational paper explicitly retains unspecified proof validation and the ordering of ordinary penalties against same-round leak confiscation. No rule was invented to close those gaps.

The numerical checks validate the stated calculations and prescribed-strategy scenarios. They do not prove truthful incentives in the two-asset design, identify equilibrium behavior, establish public benefit, or repair the historical repeated-attack balance diagnostic. That diagnostic remains labeled and excluded from payoff interpretation. Human source-verification flags remain pending, and metadata verification is not a claim that every cited work was read in full.

Continuous integration (CI) remains a separate follow-up requiring authorization to modify enforcement surfaces. The existing HTML publication and agent-setup workflows install direct requirements rather than the hashed lock and use mutable action tags. The publication workflow combines pull-request rendering and deployment in a job with pull-request and deployment write permissions. These workflows were inspected but not modified. The local renderer also differs from the configured CI version, Quarto 1.9.37; cross-version byte identity is not claimed.

The host's pip configuration supplies a release-age value that its installed pip rejects. The documented uv alternative installed and checked the locked environment without disabling the age gate or hash checks. The local prototype directory contains generated assets and installed dependencies without a reconstructible source manifest; it was preserved and is not evidence of a reproducible application build.

The branch was already diverged from its remote. The upstream clarification that the companion's count of 50 belongs to its initial relocation snapshot was incorporated; this review does not merge, rebase or rewrite that history.
