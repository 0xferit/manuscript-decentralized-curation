## Scope and limitations

The mutable UMA and Reality.eth entries intentionally have no publication year: the inspected pages did not establish one. Their access dates describe this review, not publication. The historical date-like citation keys are retained so the manuscript's references continue to resolve.

The manuscript's uses of these three sources remain the same: they illustrate optimistic or escalating dispute patterns for on-chain questions. The metadata repairs add no assertion about adjudication reliability, economic security or empirical performance. The Schelling correction selects the cited edition; the Juniper correction identifies the linked report and does not introduce any of its numerical estimates into the paper.

Some Crossref requests returned rate-limit responses; publisher records supplied the fallback where needed. The original Ohio State download, Harvard publisher page and direct Zenodo landing page were intermittently inaccessible or required browser features. Author-hosted article copies, explicit catalog records and the DataCite registration supplied the stated alternatives. A successful metadata lookup does not imply that the full text was read in this pass.

This review covers fields supplied by the identified records, not every possible bibliographic field. It does not replace the companion paper's separate claim-to-evidence records or change their human-verification status.
